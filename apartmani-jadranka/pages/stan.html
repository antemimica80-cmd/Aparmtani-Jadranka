About Stan

🛏️ Sleeps up to 4

Stan is our cozy ground-floor apartment, combining a comfortable open living area with a fully equipped kitchen and a private terrace where you can enjoy your morning coffee in the sea breeze. It's an easy walk to the beach and to local restaurants and shops in Mimice.

The apartment comprises one bedroom with a double bed and a living room with a sofa bed, making it flexible for two to four guests. Bright, quiet, and thoughtfully furnished — Stan is designed to feel like a home away from home.
