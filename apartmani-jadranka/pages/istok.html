About Istok

🛏️ Sleeps up to 6

Named for the east-facing light that fills it each morning, Istok is our larger apartment — ideal for families or small groups. It offers two bedrooms, a spacious living and dining area, and a balcony with a glimpse of the sea in the distance.

Istok sits just steps from Stan, sharing the same peaceful garden and easy access to parking, making it easy to book both units together for larger group getaways.
